iOS 27 'iPhone Handoff' Lets You Use One Number Across Two iPhones

📅 September 3, 2026 📱 iOS 27 🔋 eSIM / Carrier Support
📱 iPhone Handoff setup screen as seen in Xcode 27 — choose your main and companion device to share one phone number.

Apple is finally solving a long-standing pain point for multi-iPhone users. With the upcoming iOS 27 release, the company is introducing a groundbreaking feature called "iPhone Handoff" — a cellular sharing system that allows two iPhones to share a single phone number seamlessly. First teased briefly at WWDC 2026, newly uncovered developer demonstrations from Xcode 27 have now revealed exactly how it works.

📱 One number, two iPhones: Apple's new iPhone Handoff feature lets you keep the same phone number across your main and companion iPhone. Switch between devices anytime — calls, texts, and data follow you.

What Is iPhone Handoff?

iPhone Handoff is a new feature in iOS 27 that allows users to share a single phone number across two iPhones simultaneously. It works similarly to how an Apple Watch with cellular shares your iPhone's number, but now extended to a second iPhone.

The feature was briefly mentioned on a slide during the WWDC 2026 keynote in June, but Apple never provided details at the time. Developer demonstrations have now revealed that the feature lives in the Settings app under Cellular, where users can designate one iPhone as the main device and the other as a companion.

How iPhone Handoff Works

The setup process lives in the Settings app under Cellular. During setup, you choose one iPhone as the main device (the one you use most often) and the other as a companion device.

Apple explains that your primary eSIM stays on the main iPhone, while the companion iPhone receives a companion eSIM linked to the same number. This enables you to switch between both devices while keeping the same phone number active.

  • Cellular settings — All network configurations are managed from the main iPhone.
  • Location sharing — Automatically follows whichever iPhone is actively using the number.
  • Dynamic Island notification — Users see a confirmation when unlocking the secondary device to transfer the number.

Carrier Support Is Key

iPhone Handoff isn't just a software trick — it requires carrier support to function. Since the feature relies on companion eSIM profiles managed by network providers, not all carriers will support it at launch.

Early reports suggest that T-Mobile in the U.S. and Deutsche Telekom in Germany will likely be among the first carriers to offer the feature. Other carriers are expected to follow, but availability will depend on regional network configurations.

Why iPhone Handoff Matters

For users who juggle two iPhones — perhaps a personal and a work device, or a newer model alongside an older one for travel and exercise — this feature eliminates the need for a second SIM card or separate plan.

Beyond convenience, the feature is also seen as groundwork for Apple's rumored foldable iPhone, potentially allowing users to seamlessly switch between a compact phone and a foldable tablet-sized device.

When Will iPhone Handoff Arrive?

iOS 27 is expected to be released to the public later this month following approximately three months of beta testing. While the feature has been demonstrated in developer tools, its final implementation will depend on carrier readiness.

Apple has not yet announced pricing or whether the companion eSIM will be offered free or as a paid add-on — similar to how carriers currently handle Apple Watch cellular plans.
